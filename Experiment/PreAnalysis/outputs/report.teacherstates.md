# Cost(beta) pre-analysis

Teacher Qwen3-14B (thinking), anchor Qwen3-1.7B-Base, MATH train. 1500 states sampled from 200 rollouts generated by the teacher (Qwen3-14B, thinking), median length 1024 tokens. 19 beta values.

## Rollout statistics (the teacher (Qwen3-14B, thinking))

- 200/200 rollouts are real solution attempts; 0 (0.0%) degenerate into a few junk tokens and stop.
- 200 (100.0%) hit the 1024-token cap; length percentiles 25/50/75 = 1024/1024/1024.

Training-weighted Cost(0.4) = 0.674 nats, i.e. the target that distillation chases sits e^0.674 = 1.96x off the teacher per token on average.

Position 0 alone carries 2% of the total cost at beta=0.4 while being 0.1% of the visited positions: the teacher opens deterministically with `<think>` and the anchor never does, so the floor pays for a format token rather than a reasoning alternative.

## Format retention at the opening state

The teacher opens deterministically with `<think>`; the anchor never does. q*(teacher argmax) is the probability the distilled target leaves on that token.

| beta | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.4 | 0.8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| q*(`<think>`) | 0.900 | 0.800 | 0.600 | 0.200 |

The relation is exactly 1 - beta, and it is analytic rather than empirical: where the teacher is deterministic on a token the anchor ignores, every other token binds, so the floor moves exactly beta of the mass off the teacher's choice. A distilled student that reaches this target opens in the teacher's format 1 - beta of the time.
