# Cost(beta) pre-analysis

Teacher Qwen3-14B (thinking), anchor Qwen3-1.7B-Base, MATH train. 4000 states sampled from 800 rollouts generated by the anchor (Qwen3-1.7B-Base), median length 468 tokens. 19 beta values.

## Rollout statistics (the anchor (Qwen3-1.7B-Base))

- 779/800 rollouts are real solution attempts; 21 (2.6%) degenerate into a few junk tokens and stop.
- 125 (15.6%) hit the 1024-token cap; length percentiles 25/50/75 = 295/468/775.

Training-weighted Cost(0.4) = 0.114 nats, i.e. the target that distillation chases sits e^0.114 = 1.12x off the teacher per token on average.

Position 0 alone carries 19% of the total cost at beta=0.4 while being 0.2% of the visited positions: the teacher opens deterministically with `<think>` and the anchor never does, so the floor pays for a format token rather than a reasoning alternative.

## Format retention at the opening state

The teacher opens deterministically with `<think>`; the anchor never does. q*(teacher argmax) is the probability the distilled target leaves on that token.

| beta | 0.1 | 0.2 | 0.4 | 0.8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| q*(`<think>`) | 0.900 | 0.800 | 0.600 | 0.200 |

The relation is exactly 1 - beta, and it is analytic rather than empirical: where the teacher is deterministic on a token the anchor ignores, every other token binds, so the floor moves exactly beta of the mass off the teacher's choice. A distilled student that reaches this target opens in the teacher's format 1 - beta of the time.
